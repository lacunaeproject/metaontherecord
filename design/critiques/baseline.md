## Top three
1. The penalties grid compares 17 years of penalties (mixing $ and €, and including $12.1B not yet paid) with one year of revenue, and states no finding.
2. The entries-per-year strip reads as a rising rate of wrongdoing, but it counts this site's entries; 2026 is a partial year with no note, and there is no source line.
3. The tally breakdown lives only on hover or tap; the timeline's scroll fill encodes no data, never reaches its end state under reduced motion, and borrows the evidence glyphs' filled-means-stronger vocabulary.
