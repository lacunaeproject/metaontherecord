Meta’s own research on teen girls leaks

Reported. Documented by journalists, researchers or Meta’s own files, with no legal finding. How evidence is labeled

Internal slides said Instagram made body-image issues worse for one in three teen girls who already had them. Among teens who reported suicidal thoughts, 13% of British and 6% of American users traced them to Instagram.

Meta’s responseMeta said the reporting mischaracterized research that, in some cases, relied on input from 40 teens.
