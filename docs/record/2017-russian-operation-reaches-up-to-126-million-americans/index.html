Russian operation reaches up to 126 million Americans

Admitted. Meta acknowledged it, apologized, or published it itself. How evidence is labeled

Facebook told Congress that Russia’s Internet Research Agency published about 80,000 posts between June 2015 and August 2017, mostly on divisive subjects such as race and guns. About 29 million people were served the posts directly; sharing spread them further.
