Jury finds Instagram’s design harmed a young user

Ruled, under appeal. A court or regulator found against Meta, and Meta is appealing. How evidence is labeled

A Los Angeles jury found Meta and YouTube negligent in designing platforms that a young woman used compulsively from childhood, contributing to her depression and anxiety. The jury assigned Meta 70% of the responsibility.

Meta’s responseMeta said teen mental health is complex and cannot be linked to a single app.
