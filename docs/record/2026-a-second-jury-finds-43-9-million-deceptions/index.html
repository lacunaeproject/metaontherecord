A second jury finds 43.9 million deceptions

Ruled. A court or regulator found against Meta. How evidence is labeled

Judge Francis Mathew will set the penalty, up to $5,000 per violation.

In a case New Mexico filed in 2021 over Cambridge Analytica, a Santa Fe jury found that 26 of 29 public statements by Meta and its executives about user data, misinformation, hate speech and equal enforcement were willfully deceptive. The jury sided with the state on 31 of 34 questions.

In their words“We disagree with the verdict and will continue to defend ourselves against efforts to distort our record.” (Meta, 2026)
