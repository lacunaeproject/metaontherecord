AI chatbots allowed romantic talk with children

Admitted. Meta acknowledged it, apologized, or published it itself. How evidence is labeled

Reuters obtained Meta’s internal rules for its AI chatbots, approved by legal, policy and engineering staff including its chief ethicist. They permitted the bots to engage children in romantic or sensual conversations. Meta confirmed the document and removed those sections after Reuters asked about them.

Meta’s responseMeta said the examples were “erroneous and inconsistent with our policies.”
