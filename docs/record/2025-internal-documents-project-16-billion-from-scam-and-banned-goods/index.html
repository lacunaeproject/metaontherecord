Internal documents project $16 billion from scam and banned-goods ads

Reported. Documented by journalists, researchers or Meta’s own files, with no legal finding. How evidence is labeled

Reuters reported that Meta projected about 10% of its 2024 revenue would come from ads for scams and banned goods, and that it banned advertisers only when it was 95% certain of fraud. The reporting won a Pulitzer Prize.

Meta’s responseMeta called the 10% figure an overly inclusive estimate and says it removed 159 million scam ads in 2025.
