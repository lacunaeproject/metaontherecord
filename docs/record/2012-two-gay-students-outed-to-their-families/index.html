October 2012

Two gay students outed to their families

Reported. Documented by journalists, researchers or Meta’s own files, with no legal finding. How evidence is labeled

Bobbi Duncan and Taylor McCormick at UT-Austin had set their privacy controls so their fathers would not learn they were gay. When the Queer Chorus president added them to the choir’s Facebook group, Facebook announced it to all their friends. Any user could be added to a group without approving it.

In their words“Their unfortunate experience reminds us that we must continue our work to empower and educate users about our robust privacy controls.” (Facebook, 2012)
