A former executive’s memoir alleges help for China’s censors

Alleged. Claims in a pending case or a preliminary regulatory finding. How evidence is labeled

In Careless People, former public policy director Sarah Wynn-Williams alleges Meta built censorship tools while courting the Chinese market. An emergency arbitrator, citing her severance agreement’s non-disparagement clause, barred her from promoting the book. The order did not address whether her claims were true.

Meta’s responseMeta called the book “false and defamatory.”
