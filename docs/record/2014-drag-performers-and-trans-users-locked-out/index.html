Drag performers and trans users locked out

Admitted. Meta acknowledged it, apologized, or published it itself. How evidence is labeled

Facebook suspended drag performers and transgender users who did not use their legal names, after one person reported several hundred of their accounts as fake. Chief product officer Chris Cox apologized on October 1, 2014, and Facebook changed how the policy was enforced.

In their wordsCox apologized “for the hardship that we’ve put you through.” (Chris Cox, 2014)
